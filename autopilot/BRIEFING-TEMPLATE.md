# BRIEFING-TEMPLATE — what the owner reads

> **Guidance.** Written at the end of a run from the facts of that run. Business first, not activity. Only claim
> "done" with proof from TASK-QUEUE. Label numbers as data and say how fresh they are. An empty section says
> "Nothing." Daily: max 200 words. Weekly (Level 3): max 450 words. Same four headings every time.
> **Usefulness test** before saving: could Sam act on this in two minutes without opening another file?

## Daily briefing — <YYYY-MM-DD> <(DRY RUN) if applicable>
### What changed
- <verified change in the world, with link and task ID — or "Nothing changed in the business today" + why>
### What we learned
- Data: <fact, source, date> → Interpretation: <meaning, or "too early">
### Next best move
- <the one or two highest-value things for the next run, and why>
### Owner needed
- <decision-ready question (ESCALATION format), or "Nothing needed">

## Weekly briefing — week <nn> (Level 3; adds three lines)
- **KPIs:** task done · intermediate result · commercial outcome — this week vs last week vs target.
- **Hypotheses:** verdicts (SUPPORTED / NOT SUPPORTED / NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE) and what follows.
- **Strategy check:** did a review trigger fire? Proposed changes for approval.
