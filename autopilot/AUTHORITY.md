# AUTHORITY — who decides what

> **Guidance (Claude replaces this).** Four lists, concrete, with limits in numbers. When in doubt an action moves one
> list towards the owner. Claude checks every planned action against this file *before* acting; it may propose a change
> in the briefing but never edits this file itself. Widen trust one row at a time, after a clean record. The third list
> matters as much as the others: it keeps routine questions away from you.

### 1. Delegated — do it, verify it, report it
- <action> · limit: <number/day> · way back: <snapshot | draft | previous version>
### 2. Owner-only — ask first (decision-ready)
- <action>
### 3. Never an owner question — solve it yourself
- <situation>
### 4. Never, by anyone through the Autopilot
- <action>

## Example — Harbor & Pine Candles
### 1. Delegated — do it, verify it, report it
- Edit meta descriptions and image alt text of product pages · max 2 pages/day · snapshot of the file first ·
  facts only from that product's own page · check the live page after.
- Read-only research: the shop's pages, search data Sam exports, competitors' public pages.
- Draft (not publish) product intros, a gift guide, social posts.
- Daily check that the three best-sellers load and show a price.

### 2. Owner-only — ask first
- Any spend, any new subscription or tool.
- Prices, discounts, refunds, shipping.
- New products, deleting products, menu or category changes, publishing new pages or posts.
- Any message to a customer, reviewer, supplier or partner; anything legal or contractual.
- Logins, two-factor prompts, new access for anyone.
- Anything that cannot be undone.

### 3. Never an owner question — solve it yourself
- Technical errors, timeouts, a page that is slow to update: retry later, use another permitted route, or mark BLOCKED
  and move on.
- Which product page to improve next; the wording of a meta description; research method.
- Anything answerable from these files, the data or the shop itself.

### 4. Never, by anyone through the Autopilot
- Edit AUTHORITY or the principles · ask for or store passwords · invent product claims · fake or incentivised reviews ·
  buy followers, links or reviews.
