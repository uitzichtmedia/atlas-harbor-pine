# AUTHORITY — who decides what

Claude checks every planned action against this file before acting. It may propose changes in the briefing but never
edits this file itself.

### 1. Delegated — do it, verify it, report it
- Edit meta descriptions and image alt text of the 8 product pages · max 2 pages/day · previous text kept (git history
  plus a note in the briefing) · facts only from that product's own page · rebuild and check the built page after.
  *Starts only after the first task is approved (see TASK-QUEUE).*
- Read-only research: the shop's pages, files you export (orders, search data), competitors' public pages.
- Daily check that Pine Forest Soy Candle, Amber Evening and Orange & Clove load and show a price.
- Write drafts, audits and briefings inside the Autopilot folder (nothing published).

### 2. Owner-only — ask first (decision-ready)
- Any spend above €50/month, any new subscription or tool.
- Prices, discounts, refunds, shipping.
- New or deleted products; publishing new pages or posts (including a gift guide).
- Any message to a customer, reviewer, supplier or marketplace.
- Anything legal or contractual.
- Logins, two-factor prompts, new access for anyone.
- Changing anything outside meta descriptions and alt text; anything that cannot be undone.

### 3. Never an owner question — solve it yourself
- Technical errors, build problems, timeouts: retry, use another permitted route, or mark BLOCKED and take the next task.
- Which product page to look at next; the wording of a meta description; research method.
- Anything answerable from these files, the data or the shop itself.

### 4. Never, by anyone through the Autopilot
- Edit AUTHORITY or the principles · ask for or store passwords · invent product claims (burn time, ingredients,
  safety) · fake or incentivised reviews · buy followers, links or reviews · push to `main`.
